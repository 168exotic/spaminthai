# Feature graphic brief — 1024 × 500

Play **requires** one feature graphic (the banner at the top of your store page and in promos). Without it you cannot publish.

## Hard specs
- **Size:** exactly **1024 × 500 px**
- **Format:** PNG or JPEG, 24-bit, **no alpha/transparency**
- **No important content in the outer ~10%** (Play may crop/overlay a play button)
- Text should be readable as a thumbnail — keep it big and short

## What to convey
A trustworthy, modern Thai anti-scam shield. One glance = "this app tells me who's calling and blocks scammers."

## Layout suggestion
- **Left third:** app icon or a shield + phone icon.
- **Center/right:** headline + one line of support text.
- **Background:** rose gradient (brand **#F43F5E** → deeper `#BE123C`), or white with rose accents. Keep high contrast.

## Copy options (pick one — keep it SHORT)
Thai:
```
เช็คเบอร์ใคร · บล็อกมิจฉาชีพ
ฟรี ไม่ต้องสมัคร — ฐานข้อมูลคนไทย
```
English (for en-US listing variant):
```
Know who's calling. Block the scammers.
Free · Community-powered
```

## Colors & fonts
- Primary: **#F43F5E** (rose). Deep accent: **#BE123C**. Ink text: **#0F172A** or white.
- Font: a bold Thai-capable font — **IBM Plex Sans Thai** (matches the app/site) or Prompt/Kanit (bold weight).

## How to produce it
**Option A — DIY in Canva (free, ~15 min):**
1. Canva → Custom size → 1024 × 500 px.
2. Search template "app feature graphic" / "banner", pick a clean one.
3. Apply rose #F43F5E, drop in the icon + one headline above.
4. Download as PNG (flatten, no transparency).

**Option B — Hire a designer (~$8–15):**
- Fiverr: search "Google Play feature graphic" — many gigs at $10, 24–48h delivery.
- Brief to give them: this file (specs + copy + colors + the app icon PNG).

## Deliverable
Save as `feature-graphic-1024x500.png` and upload under **Store listing → Graphics → Feature graphic**.

> Also required elsewhere: **App icon 512×512** (32-bit PNG with alpha). If you don't have a hi-res icon, export it from the Android project's `ic_launcher` at 512×512 or recreate in Canva with the same rose theme.
